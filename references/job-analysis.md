# Job description analysis

Use the user-provided posting to understand what the employer is seeking. The posting is not evidence that the candidate has any qualification.

## Extract and prioritize

Identify:

- Target title, level, team, and domain.
- Responsibilities and expected outcomes.
- Required qualifications and preferred qualifications.
- Technologies, methods, and relevant terminology.
- Explicit constraints such as location or work authorization, if relevant to the user's requested resume.

Separate explicit requirements from implied preferences. Preserve the employer's meaning when normalizing terms, and note ambiguity rather than resolving it by assumption. Distinguish a required qualification from an item that appears only in a general description or list of examples.

## Match by experience

For each material requirement, compare the actual experience described in the resume and portfolio:

- **Direct match:** specific evidence demonstrates the stated skill or responsibility.
- **Transferable or adjacent:** related experience exists, but does not establish the exact tool, environment, or scope requested.
- **Unclear:** evidence is too broad or ambiguous to determine fit.
- **No evidence found:** reviewed sources do not support the requirement. This is not proof the candidate lacks the experience.

Recognize synonyms and equivalent concepts only when the evidence supports the relationship. For example, experience operating a managed Kubernetes service may support Kubernetes experience, but must not be rewritten as experience with another cloud provider.

## Use keywords accurately

Use relevant posting terminology in the resume only when it accurately describes documented experience. Do not stuff keywords, add unsupported skills to a skills list, or present the employer's wording as candidate evidence. Tailor emphasis and phrasing, not facts.

## Boundaries

Analyze the supplied job description or user-provided URL only. Do not perform additional company research, infer hidden requirements, or predict screening outcomes. If the posting is incomplete or inaccessible, ask for pasted text or note the limitation.
